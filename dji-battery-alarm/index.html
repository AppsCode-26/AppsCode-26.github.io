Charge alarm

Point your phone at a DJI charging hub. It rings when a battery reaches the number of lights you pick.

  1. Prop your phone so the camera looks straight at the hub's lights, close up.
  2. Tap Start and allow the camera.
  3. Leave this page open. It rings when a battery gets to your level.

Sound

Plays at your phone's media volume, even on silent. Turn it up and tap Test sound to check.

More settings

Off: a light counts once it stops blinking and stays on. On: it counts as soon as it starts blinking.

Light detection

Pick Sensitive if it misses lights that are on. Pick Strict if it sees lights that are off.
