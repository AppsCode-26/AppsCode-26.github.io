## Games

- [Tilt Racer](tilt-racer/) – 3D driving game for iPad with gyroscope (tilt) steering, free roam, dirt racing against AI and an upgradeable garage.

## Tools

- [DJI Charge Alarm](dji-battery-alarm/) – watches a DJI charging hub's battery lights through the phone camera and rings when a battery reaches the number of lights you pick (2 by default).
